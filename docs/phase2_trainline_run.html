TrainLineAI — first campaign run

12 attempts, 4 seeds × 3 rank combinations each, run through the revised sign format (town_a_rank/town_b_rank/engine_rank/line_index), simulated for 3 in-game years. Construction status/cost was read back from the savegame's SIGN chunk (not AI log output — confirms the two-panel format actually lands in the data, at the 31-character limit that silently broke the old one-panel format); profit_ligne for each built line was assembled from VEHS (operating profit of the lead vehicles) minus construction cost amortized on two horizons: vehicle cost over the rolling stock's own max_age, infrastructure cost (track, bridges/tunnels, stations, depot) over an assumed 30-year planning horizon — OpenTTD gives infrastructure no lifespan of its own to derive one from.
