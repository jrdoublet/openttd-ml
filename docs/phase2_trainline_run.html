TrainLineAI — campaign run

18 attempts, 6 seeds × 3 engine ranks each, simulated for the full 10 in-game years — first re-baseline since the town-pair/station/depot rewrite (commits 88a788f/4613ef3/37db73b): town pairs are now chosen automatically (population×population/distance score) and validated with a real pathfinder search before any construction; stations are oriented from the actual track direction with a straight-run check so the platform can never pave over a bend; the depot connects only after each junction tile is verified with AIRail.AreTilesConnected. A prior navigation bug (trains stuck shuttling near the depot, never reaching the far station) and a prior loading bug (OF_NONE orders leaving wagons empty, fixed with OF_FULL_LOAD_ANY) are both resolved — see docs/methode.md. Construction status/cost/vehicle-cost came back from the savegame's SIGN chunk; profit_ligne assembled from VEHS (profit_last_year of the lead vehicles, a complete year — not profit_this_year, which can be partial) minus construction cost amortized on two horizons: vehicle cost over the rolling stock's own max_age, infrastructure cost over an assumed 30-year planning horizon. First real signal: revenue is no longer stuck at zero — profit_ligne now varies genuinely by run, mostly positive with a couple of negative outliers, rather than being a uniform running-cost-only number.
